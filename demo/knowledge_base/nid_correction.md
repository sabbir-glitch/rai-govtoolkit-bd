TITLE: National ID (NID) Correction

SUMMARY: How to correct an error on your National ID card (e.g. wrong name spelling, date of birth, or father's/mother's name).

STEPS:
1. Log in to the Election Commission's NID online portal (services.nidw.gov.bd) using your existing NID number.
2. Go to "Edit" and select the field you want to correct.
3. Upload supporting documents proving the correct information (see below).
4. Pay the applicable correction fee online.
5. Visit your local Upazila/District Election Office if biometric re-verification is required, or if the portal directs you to in-person verification.
6. Track your application status online; corrections are typically processed within 15-30 working days, though this can vary by office workload.

REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS:
- Original NID card
- Birth certificate (for name/DOB corrections)
- Educational certificate (SSC or equivalent, if available, as supporting evidence)
- A general diary (GD) copy if the correction involves a significant discrepancy

FEE: A correction fee applies, varying by the type and number of fields corrected; the current fee schedule is published on the NID portal.

WHERE_TO_APPLY: Online via services.nidw.gov.bd, with in-person follow-up at your local Upazila/District Election Office if required.

SOURCE: Election Commission Secretariat, NID correction circular (reference to be confirmed against the current official notice at time of use).

NOTE: This is illustrative content for a governance-toolkit demo. Do not rely on the fee, timeline, or document list above for an actual NID correction — always confirm against the live NID portal or your local Election Office.
