TITLE: Trade Licence Application

SUMMARY: How to apply for or renew a trade licence for a small business.

STEPS:
1. Apply online through your local City Corporation / Municipality / Union Parishad e-service portal, or in person at the relevant Trade Licence office.
2. Provide business details: business name, type of trade, address, and owner's NID.
3. Submit supporting documents (see below).
4. Pay the trade licence fee, which varies by business category and location (fee schedules differ between City Corporations and Municipalities).
5. Collect the licence certificate; renewal is typically required annually.

REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS:
- Owner's National ID card
- Proof of business address (rental agreement or ownership document)
- Passport-size photographs
- TIN (Tax Identification Number) certificate, for certain business categories

FEE: Varies by business category, size, and the issuing City Corporation/Municipality; fee schedules are published locally.

WHERE_TO_APPLY: Local City Corporation, Municipality, or Union Parishad Trade Licence office, or the relevant online e-service portal where available.

SOURCE: Relevant City Corporation/Municipality Trade Licence circular (reference to be confirmed against the current official notice at time of use).

NOTE: This is illustrative content for a governance-toolkit demo. Do not rely on the fee or document list above for an actual application — always confirm against your local City Corporation/Municipality office.
