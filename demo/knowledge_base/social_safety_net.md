TITLE: Social Safety Net Programme Eligibility (Old Age Allowance example)

SUMMARY: General information on eligibility criteria and how to apply for the Old Age Allowance programme. This entry illustrates how the bot explains eligibility criteria WITHOUT making an eligibility determination itself.

STEPS:
1. Check the published eligibility criteria (age, income, and other conditions) on the Department of Social Services portal (dss.gov.bd) or with your local Union Parishad/Ward office.
2. Collect the application form from your local Union Parishad, Ward office, or the online portal where available.
3. Submit the form along with required documents to the local Union Parishad committee responsible for beneficiary selection.
4. Applications are reviewed by a local selection committee against the published criteria and budget allocation for your area.
5. Approved beneficiaries are added to the payment list and receive payments through the designated channel (often mobile financial services or bank).

REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS:
- National ID card
- Proof of age (NID or birth certificate)
- Proof of income/economic status, if requested by the local committee

FEE: No application fee.

WHERE_TO_APPLY: Local Union Parishad or Ward Office; some areas also offer online pre-registration via dss.gov.bd.

IMPORTANT_BOUNDARY: This chatbot explains criteria and the application process only. It does NOT determine whether any individual is eligible — eligibility is decided by the local selection committee based on the published criteria, local budget allocation, and verification. Any query asking "am I eligible?" should be answered with the general criteria plus a direction to apply through the local Union Parishad, not a yes/no determination.

SOURCE: Department of Social Services, Old Age Allowance programme circular (reference to be confirmed against the current official notice at time of use).

NOTE: This is illustrative content for a governance-toolkit demo. Do not rely on the details above for an actual application — always confirm against dss.gov.bd or your local Union Parishad office.
