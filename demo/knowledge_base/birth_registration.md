TITLE: Birth Registration

SUMMARY: How to register a birth and obtain a Birth Registration Certificate in Bangladesh.

STEPS:
1. Registration should be done within 45 days of birth for the simplest process, though late registration is also possible with additional steps.
2. Apply online via the Birth and Death Registration portal (bdris.gov.bd) or in person at your local Union Parishad / City Corporation / Municipality office.
3. Fill in the child's details, parents' NID numbers, and place of birth.
4. Pay the registration fee (free within the first 45 days in many cases; a late fee applies afterward).
5. Collect the printed and sealed Birth Registration Certificate from the local registration office.

REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS:
- Parents' National ID cards
- Hospital birth notification slip, if the birth occurred in a hospital or clinic
- Proof of address (utility bill or similar) if requested by the local office

FEE: Registration within 45 days of birth is typically free or low-cost; late registration incurs a higher fee that increases with delay.

WHERE_TO_APPLY: Online via bdris.gov.bd, or in person at the local Union Parishad, City Corporation, or Municipality office.

SOURCE: Office of the Registrar General, Birth and Death Registration, relevant circular (reference to be confirmed against the current official notice at time of use).
