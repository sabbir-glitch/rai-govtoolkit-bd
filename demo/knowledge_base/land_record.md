TITLE: Land Record (Khatian) Check

SUMMARY: How to check your land record (khatian/porcha) status and obtain a certified copy.

STEPS:
1. Visit the national land service portal (land.gov.bd) or the e-Porcha service.
2. Search using mouza name, khatian number, or the landowner's name.
3. View the record online for basic verification.
4. Apply for a certified copy if needed for a legal or transaction purpose, paying the applicable fee online.
5. Collect the certified copy from the relevant Assistant Commissioner (Land) office if in-person collection is required, or receive it digitally where that service is available.

REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS:
- Mouza name and khatian/plot number (or landowner's name and NID for a search)
- Applicant's NID, if applying for a certified copy

FEE: A nominal fee applies for a certified copy; basic online search may be free.

WHERE_TO_APPLY: Online via land.gov.bd / e-Porcha, or in person at the local Assistant Commissioner (Land) / Union Land Office.

SOURCE: Ministry of Land, e-Porcha service circular (reference to be confirmed against the current official notice at time of use).

NOTE: This is illustrative content for a governance-toolkit demo. Do not rely on the process details above for an actual land record request — always confirm against land.gov.bd or your local Land Office.
